Privacy policy

How we collect, use and protect your personal and health information.

Last updated: November 2026

Our commitment

Mill Pond Dental Care respects your privacy. We collect, use and share personal health information only as needed to provide your dental care, and in line with Ontario’s Personal Health Information Protection Act (PHIPA).

What we collect

  • Contact details, such as your name, address, phone number and email
  • Health and dental history, treatment records and X-rays
  • Insurance and billing information
  • Messages you send us through this website

How we use it

  • To provide, plan and coordinate your dental care
  • To book and remind you of appointments
  • To send claims and estimates to your insurer or government plan
  • To meet our legal and professional obligations

Sharing your information

We share your information only with people involved in your care (for example, a specialist we refer you to), your insurer or government plan when you ask us to, or when the law requires it. We never sell your information.

This website

Please don’t send detailed medical information through our website form; we’ll talk about it in person or by phone. The website may use basic analytics to understand how it’s used. Our Google reviews badge and map are provided by third parties, which may set their own cookies.

Your rights

You may ask to see or correct your records, or withdraw consent for certain uses, by contacting us. If you have a concern, you can also contact the Information and Privacy Commissioner of Ontario.
