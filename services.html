Coverage made simple

We accept the Canadian Dental Care Plan and work with most insurance providers. Every mouth is different, so we don’t publish a price list: after your exam you’ll get a written estimate before any work begins.

Ask about your coverage

  • CDCP accepted

    Covered by the Canadian Dental Care Plan? You’re welcome here.

  • Direct billing

    We send claims to most insurers, so you pay only your share.

  • Payment options

    Debit, credit and cash. Ask us about larger treatments.
