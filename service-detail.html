What is family dentistry?

Mill Pond Dental Care is a family-focused general dental practice. We offer services in all areas of dentistry for children, adults and seniors, so everyone in your household can see the same familiar team.

Think of your dentist as the team captain for your oral health. We look after your check-ups, cleanings and everyday treatment, and when you need more specialised care, we coordinate it through our network of dental specialists.

Who is it for?

Anyone looking for a regular dental home in Dorchester and Thames Centre, including:

  • Children, from their very first visit
  • Teens and adults due for a check-up and cleaning
  • Seniors, including care for dentures
  • Families who would like appointments booked back to back
  • Patients covered by the Canadian Dental Care Plan (CDCP)

Benefits

  • One familiar team for every age. Book the whole family with people who already know your history.
  • Problems found early. Regular check-ups help us spot issues while they are small and easier to manage.
  • Oral cancer screening at every check-up. A quick, routine part of your exam.
  • Specialist care, coordinated for you. If you need a specialist, we arrange it and stay involved.
  • Clear costs before treatment. You’ll get a written estimate before any work begins.

Questions

Family dentistry FAQs

Do you see children?

Yes. We see patients of every age, from children to seniors. Ask us when to book your child’s first visit and we’ll help you plan it.

Can my whole family book at the same time?

Yes, we can book family members back to back. Call reception at 519-268-3351 and we’ll find times that work for everyone.

Do you accept the Canadian Dental Care Plan?

Yes. We accept the Canadian Dental Care Plan (CDCP) and bill most private insurers directly, so you pay only your share.

What happens at a check-up?

A full exam, any X-rays you need, a cleaning with one of our hygienists and an oral cancer screening. We’ll talk you through anything we find before planning next steps.

What if I need a specialist?

Your dentist will explain why, then coordinate your care through our network of dental specialists, so you’re never left to figure it out alone.

When are you open?

Monday to Thursday, 8 a.m. to 5:30 p.m. We also offer occasional Friday hygiene appointments from 8 a.m. to 2 p.m.
