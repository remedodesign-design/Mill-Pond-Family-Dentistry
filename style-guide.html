10

Naming rules

Client-First in short, for everyone building this site.

  • ✓Custom classes use an underscore: service-card_component, home-hero_image. The part before the underscore is the folder.
  • ✓Utility classes use dashes only: text-size-small, margin-bottom.
  • ✓Combo classes start with is- and sit on a base class: button is-secondary, logo_component is-stacked.
  • ✓Sections: section_[page-or-purpose], for example section_new-patients-steps.
  • ✓Full words, lowercase, dashes between words. No abbreviations.
  • ✕No hex values or font names typed into a class. Use the variables.
  • ✕No margins on headings, text or components. Wrap and use spacing classes.
  • ✕No text-transform: capitalize. It breaks CDCP, Dr. and Invisalign.
  • ✕No “Div Block 12” or “Heading 3”. Every element gets a real class or none.
  • ✕No combo classes more than two deep, except the button alternates.

11

Rich text

One class, text-rich-text, on every Webflow Rich Text element and CMS rich text field: blog posts, service pages, the new-patient page and policies. Place it inside container-small so lines stay near 65 characters. Editors only pick the tag; the class does the rest.

What to expect at your first visit

Your first appointment at Mill Pond Dental Care takes about an hour. We'll get to know you, look at your teeth and gums, and talk through anything that worries you before we suggest a plan. There's no pressure to book treatment on the day.

If you're nervous about the dentist, tell us when you book. We can go at your pace, explain each step before we start, and talk about sedation options if they'd help.

Before you come in

A few things make the visit quicker:

  • Your insurance card, or your Canadian Dental Care Plan member ID
  • A list of medications you take, including vitamins
  • Any X-rays from your last dentist, taken in the past two years
    • We can request these for you with your consent
    • Digital copies by email are fine

During the appointment

  1. We review your health history and ask what you'd like help with.
  2. We take X-rays and a full set of photos.
  3. The dentist checks your teeth, gums, bite and jaw, and screens for oral cancer.
  4. We go over what we found and give you a written estimate for any treatment.

We bill most insurance plans directly, so you usually pay only your share on the day.
