**Contradictions / ambiguities**
1. **Date**: "Feb 09" has no year, yet the timeline says go-live "around the new year" with a "review period in January". A Feb meeting cannot lead to a New Year go-live in the same calendar year, so either the date is wrong/old, or the go-live is a year later. Confirm the real meeting date and current project clock (the date today is 6 Nov 2026).
2. **"Iichester.co.uk"**: probably a typo of Chichester (e.g. a Chichester practice) or another domain; cannot be verified from the sheet. The reference may be wrong, which blocks the design direction.
3. **"move page"**: ambiguous term (likely "mood page"/"mood board" or "mock page"). Clarify the deliverable.
4. Two reference sites named as preferred but only one chosen; unclear what to take from Meliora Dental.
5. "Associate dentist on maternity leave": unclear whether she should appear on the Team page, and with what wording.
6. "Dr. Kitty" and "Steven MacHardy": naming/credential format not given; confirm spelling and titles (DDS/DMD, etc.).
7. Timeline: 6-8 weeks from a sign-off date unknown, so the "new year" go-live may be infeasible depending on mockup approval.
8. Marketing plan not selected, yet SEO is said to be integral and simultaneous; scope of SEO work on the site build (keyword research, per-page copy, blog) depends on this decision.
