Our story

Dorchester’s neighbourhood dental office for over 40 years

Dr. Lida Kiddie opened Mill Pond Dental Care in 1982 with a simple idea: treat every patient like a neighbour. Many of the families who walked through our doors then still come to see us today, now with their children and grandchildren.

Dr. Steven MacHardy joined the practice in 2020 and carries that same unhurried, family-first approach, along with modern treatments like implants, Invisalign and IV sedation.

Coverage made simple

We accept the Canadian Dental Care Plan and work with most insurance providers. Every mouth is different, so we don’t publish a price list: after your exam you’ll get a written estimate before any work begins.

Ask about your coverage

  • CDCP accepted

    Covered by the Canadian Dental Care Plan? You’re welcome here.

  • Direct billing

    We send claims to most insurers, so you pay only your share.

  • Payment options

    Debit, credit and cash. Ask us about larger treatments.

Dental emergency?

Toothache, a broken tooth or a knocked-out tooth: call us right away rather than sending a message. After hours, our answering service will tell you what to do next.

Call now519-268-3351

Facial swelling or trouble breathing? Go to the nearest emergency room or call 911.
