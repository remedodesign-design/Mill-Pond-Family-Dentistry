Your first visit, step by step

Switching dentists or haven’t been in a while? No judgement here. This is what to expect.

  1. 1

    Book online or call

    Pick a time that suits you, or call reception and we’ll find one together.

    About 2 minutes
  2. 2

    Fill in your forms

    Send your health history before you arrive, so your visit is all about you.

    Online or at reception
  3. 3

    Your first visit

    A full exam, any X-rays you need, a cleaning and plenty of time for questions.

    About 60–90 minutes
  4. 4

    Your plan, explained

    We walk you through your options and costs. Nothing starts until you’re comfortable.

    Written estimate first

Coverage made simple

We don’t bill insurers directly, except for government plans, but we send your claims and estimates electronically on your behalf. After your exam you’ll get a written estimate before any work begins.

  • Government plans, including CDCP, billed directly
  • Claims and estimates sent electronically to most insurers
  • Debit, credit and cash

Dental emergency?

Toothache, a broken tooth or a knocked-out tooth: call us right away rather than sending a message. After hours, our answering service will tell you what to do next.

Facial swelling or trouble breathing? Go to the nearest emergency room or call 911.
