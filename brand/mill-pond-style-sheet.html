01

Brand basics

A small-town family practice that has been in Dorchester since 1982. The site should feel calm, plain and easy to use, with nothing that pops up or shouts.

Name and details

  • ✓Mill Pond Dental Care in full on first mention, in titles, schema and the footer.
  • ✓Mill Pond on its own is fine in running text after the first mention.
  • ✕Not “Mill Pond Dental 1”, “MPDC” or “Millpond” on the website. The lowercase “mpdc” mark stays for print and social avatars only.
  • ✓Phone written as 519-268-3351. Doctors written as “Dr. MacHardy”, with the period.
  • ✓Canadian spelling: colour, centre, cheque. Sentence case for headings.

Personality

SteadyClear, solid letterforms and a quiet palette. Nothing flashy.
NeighbourlySpeak like the front desk would. “We'll call you back” beats “A representative will contact you”.
ReassuringExplain what happens next. Calm blues, plenty of white space, no pressure tactics.

Rules

  • ✓Headings in sentence case. Caps only for the logo, eyebrows, nav, buttons and one short display line per page.
  • ✓Body at weight 400 or heavier. Bold is 600.
  • ✕No text-transform: capitalize. It breaks CDCP, Dr. and Invisalign.
  • ✕No condensed font for paragraphs, and no light weights (300 or below).

Webflow setup

Add from Google Fonts in Site settings → Fonts:

  • Barlow: 400, 400 italic, 500, 600

Remove Work Sans from the project so it isn't loaded.

8px buttons, inputs

Pill tags

One soft shadow only, for menus and the sticky mobile bar: 0 8px 24px rgba(31,78,121,.12). Cards use a 1px #D9DEE4 border instead.

The sign's arched top can appear once, as a curved edge on the hero or footer band. Not on every section.

Icons and photos

Line icons, 1.75px stroke at 24px, rounded ends, Deep Pond or Charcoal.

Photos: the real team, reception and the sign. Natural daylight, warm skin tones, no heavy filters. Stock only as a stopgap, and never staged “perfect smile” close-ups. No testimonials or star ratings (college rules).
